About Me


M. Raheeq is an aspiring ArchViz professional with practical experience in architectural visualization, exterior design, 2D planning, 3D modeling, and rendering. He has worked both full-time and remotely with industry professionals, gaining hands-on expertise in AutoCAD, 3ds Max, and Adobe Photoshop.

Since 2019, he has developed strong skills in creating detailed architectural designs, with a focus on exterior projects and visual presentation. His experience includes working with civil engineers and remote design professionals, expanding his knowledge in interior and exterior visualization.

Alongside his technical abilities, he is dedicated to continuous learning and professional growth. With a solid foundation in architectural design tools and real-world project experience, Raheeq is building a promising career in the ArchViz industry while striving to deliver high-quality design solutions.
